# Pinball Project Specification

[🇫🇷 French version](french-version/project-specification.md)

Our goal is to build a pinball machine with an Arduino board, motors and sensors in less than 3 months.

The aim of the game is to score as many points as possible by directing one or more metal balls on a playfield towards interactive targets. We want to bring the main components of a pinball machine into a more compact version with affordable components, to make this game accessible to as many people as possible as an open-source project. This will strengthen our knowledge through practice, by learning to use a wide variety of tools and components, and also develop our project management skills.

The main parts of our pinball machine will be:

* A wooden board.
* The flippers (small levers controlled by the player, which send the ball towards the different elements of the game).
* Buttons (to control the flippers).
* Bumpers (send the ball back in the opposite direction and add points to the player's score).
* Slingshots (bounce the ball off rubber bands fitted with mechanical levers to bring it back into the playing area).
* A spring-loaded rod with a handle (to launch the ball).
* Pass sensors (infrared sensors that tell where the ball went, to add points or trigger some game elements).
* Targets (surfaces fitted with microswitches that detect a hit to count points).
* Ramps the ball can take to score points.
* LCD display (shows the score, the number of tries left and messages about the game).
* Multiball mode (bonus releasing several balls at once from the top of the pinball machine).
* LEDs (decoration and interactivity, light up when some sensors are triggered).

Bonus if time allows: a wheel of fortune triggered at certain moments of the game (a spinning wheel that awards points depending on where it stops).
